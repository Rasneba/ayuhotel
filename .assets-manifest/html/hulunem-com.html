Ayu International Hotel sits at the heart of Adama (Nazret). The Rift Valley town of Adama is a popular weekend getaway and conference destination, and Ayu positions itself as an affordable yet well-equipped option for both business and leisure travelers. The drive from Addis Ababa takes just 40–45 minutes on the new highway express.
Rooms range from single (30 sqm) to standard (25–35 sqm), all fitted with queen beds, a coffee maker, refrigerator, TV, safety box, air conditioning, and daily cleaning service. The hotel features a swimming pool, restaurant and garden, bar, conference hall, gymnasium, sauna, beauty salon, elevator, ATM, and 24-hour front desk service. Free parking and Wi-Fi are available throughout the property.
The on-site restaurant serves traditional Ethiopian cuisine, with guests singling out the food as a highlight. The garden and outdoor terrace offer a relaxed setting with live music. The conference hall is fully equipped for workshops and corporate events. For quieter stays, guests recommend requesting a room at the back of the building, away from the main road.
